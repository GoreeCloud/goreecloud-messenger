# GoreeCloud Messenger — Competitive Objectives

Capability references include Google Messages, Samsung Messages, Signal Messenger, Telegram Messenger, WhatsApp Messenger, Session Messenger, other modern encrypted messengers, carrier messaging clients, and integrated calling applications. GoreeCloud Messenger must remain an original GoreeCloud-owned implementation; competitive inspiration does not authorize copying third-party source, private protocols, branding, trade dress, protected artwork, or product-specific UI layouts.

Objectives:

- Deliver dependable username-first encrypted Data messaging independent of cellular service.
- Converge strong consumer expectations for private/group messaging, replies/threads, editing/deletion, scheduling, drafts, reactions, forwarding, pinning, saved messages, rich media/files, search, organization, and multi-device continuity into one coherent GoreeCloud experience.
- Support one-to-one/group voice and video calling, invitation links, and supported screen sharing as native Messenger communication capabilities.
- Provide privacy-oriented features such as disappearing messages, expiration controls, view-once/self-destructing media, phone-number privacy, verification, blocking/reporting, protected local state, metadata minimization, and supported user-controlled key options without overstating enforcement.
- Match strong media/document expectations including voice messages, high-resolution media, large files, documents/PDFs, contact cards, and GoreeCloud Location sharing.
- Provide desktop, web, and multi-device continuity while preserving identity, authorization, encryption, and device-trust boundaries.
- Make Messenger a controlled first-party communications layer across GoreeCloud through Identity, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Glaze UI, Sync, Backups, Drive, Location, notifications, search/discovery, content previews, and application handoff.
- Keep the server outside plaintext Data message and attachment content wherever the cryptographic design permits.
- Prevent silent downgrade from encrypted GoreeCloud Data to SMS/MMS.
- Integrate legitimate SMS/MMS/RCS and carrier calling only through supported platform/carrier interfaces and label those transports accurately.
- Use GoreeCloud Identity for account/session/device authority and privacy-preserving username resolution.
- Ensure platform-dependent properties such as RCS interoperability, screenshot protection, view-once enforcement, and private routing are claimed only where actual implementation and runtime evidence support them.

Competitive parity and feature convergence are objectives, not present implementation claims. Stable claims require implementation, validation, integration, release, and production acceptance evidence.
